# Concise

Review all comments in all code and all markdown created in this branch:

- Ensure they are as clear and concise as they can be.
- Use ASD-STE100.
- Do NOT change the logic or meaning.
- Do NOT remove important details or rationale.

Just be as clear and concise as you can be.
